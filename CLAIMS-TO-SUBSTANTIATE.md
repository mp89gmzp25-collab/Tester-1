# CLAIMS-TO-SUBSTANTIATE.md

Every `[CITATION NEEDED — …]` marker in the copy, with its location. A claim ships only when a citation
is attached **or** the claim is softened/removed. Do not fabricate study names, authors, journals, or figures.
Dietary-supplement / structure-function framing only.

| # | Claim (topic) | Location | What's needed to substantiate | Fallback if unsupported |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Fibrinolytic (clearing-side) activity declines with age | §5.3 step 3 | A citable source that fibrinolytic capacity decreases with age | Soften to "many people find…"; remove the age-specific mechanism claim |
| 2 | Nattokinase supports fibrinolytic activity (mechanism) | §5.3 close; §5.9 | Mechanism reference for nattokinase and fibrinolysis | Keep as structure/function support statement only, no mechanism detail |
| 3 | The widely-cited null RCT used ~2,000 FU | §5.4 (the strongest argument) | The actual trial: duration, n, and **the FU/dose used** — must verify the dose figure exactly | Remove the specific FU figure; do not cite the study at all rather than misstate it |
| 4 | Nattokinase discovery attribution (found in a traditional food) | §5.6 | Discovery source/attribution | Keep the "old food, looked at closely" framing without specific researcher/date |
| 5 | Early functional timeline (weeks 1–4 onset) | §5.8; FAQ 5 | Any onset/timeline evidence | Keep as "people commonly report," no implied clinical timeline |
| 6 | Long-term circulatory / cardiovascular support | §5.8 | Structure/function support reference | Standard permitted structure/function phrasing only |
| 7 | Fibrinolytic safety profile / mechanism specificity (plaque question) | §5.9 | Safety-profile reference addressing the "supports a normal process, not a demolition" framing | Keep to "supports a normal process" + "start with your doctor"; drop any specificity claim |
| 8 | Dose threshold ("below a real dose the research doesn't apply") | §5.4; FAQ 1 | Support that studied benefits are dose-dependent above the low-FU range | Frame as "the studies were run at meaningful FU" without an implied hard cutoff |

## Regulatory note

US dietary-supplement context (DSHEA). Permitted verbs used in the copy: *supports healthy circulation,
supports normal fibrinolytic activity, supports healthy blood flow, supports cardiovascular function.*
Banned and confirmed absent: reverses plaque, unclogs arteries, removes %, treats/cures/prevents any
disease, replaces medication. A US supplement disclaimer ("This statement has not been evaluated by the
FDA…") should accompany the structure/function claims — add via a schema `richtext` disclaimer setting.
